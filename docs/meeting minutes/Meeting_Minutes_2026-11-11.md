### Workflow for pairs (agreed)
1. Create a **branch named for the use case** and open a **draft pull request** for it.
2. Request your partner as reviewer, so they get a notification.
3. The partner reviews and revises in the draft PR (comments and commits show up in its history).
4. When both partners sign off, mark the PR ready for review and **merge it into main**. A third reviewer isn't needed for now.
- Before merging, pull main into your local branch, merge it into your branch, then push, to avoid conflicts. Ask in Slack if there are problems.
- Mentioning a teammate on a commit sends a notification, but a draft PR with a requested reviewer is the better signal. The difference between "assignee" and "reviewer" wasn't settled (a reviewer signs off).
- A stray branch Cody created by mistake will be deleted.
